Astro Smart Attendance — enclosure v3, the complete re-design

110 × 155 × 46 mm · 4 printed parts (the RFID hold-down is now one ring instead of two clamp bars) · no supports · 22 circular self-tapping pilots · 167.9 cm3 of plastic (154 g printed PLA at 15 % infill) · three checkers say ALL CHECKS PASS, and the second one reads only the STL triangles.

  • 100 % open RFID scan aperture — 56 × 38 mm, R5 corners, 0 of 8269 probe rays blocked (v2 lost 190 mm2 to stiffener bars)
  • d28 fan bore with nothing across it — no grille, and the 1.4 mm seat lip v2 left standing around the bore is gone; the thinnest sheet anywhere in the print is now the 2.2 mm band the RC522 lies on
  • 0.45 × 3.0 mm rebate rings sunk around the LCD and fingerprint openings — each module's own bezel registers in them, so the openings read as a shadow line instead of a cut edge
  • rear plate: register frame, not a plug — 52.8 cm3 instead of 79.6 cm3, with the whole ESP32 zone cut clear so its bosses and locating tabs have room
  • fresh intake wall — 6 × d5.0 holes at two heights on the side opposite the fan, staggered so no hole breaks an ESP32 boss
  • every module screwed down, RC522 included: 4 × M2.5 into blind pilots, heads sunk in the ring, no glue, no clips, no snap-fits
