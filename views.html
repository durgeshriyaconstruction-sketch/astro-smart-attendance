Astro Smart Attendance — enclosure v3.4, the complete re-design

110 × 155 × 46 mm · 4 printed parts (the RFID hold-down is now one ring instead of two clamp bars) · no supports · 22 circular self-tapping pilots · 172.2 cm3 of plastic (159 g printed PLA at 15 % infill) · four checkers say ALL CHECKS PASS, and the second one reads only the STL triangles.

  • 100 % open RFID scan aperture — 56 × 38 mm, R5 corners, 0 of 8269 probe rays blocked (v2 lost 190 mm2 to stiffener bars)
  • d28 fan bore with nothing across it, and it is the only hole for air on the whole box — no grille, and the 1.4 mm seat lip v2 left standing around the bore is gone; the thinnest sheet anywhere in the print is now the 2.2 mm band the RC522 lies on
  • 0.45 × 3.0 mm rebate rings sunk around the LCD and fingerprint openings — each module's own bezel registers in them, so the openings read as a shadow line instead of a cut edge
  • rear plate: register frame, not a plug — 53.8 cm3 instead of 79.6 cm3, with the whole ESP32 zone cut clear so its bosses and locating tabs have room
  • v3.4: one hole, not twelve — asked for by name (“only let that DC fan one, not more”), the 8 bottom and 4 top grille slots are filled in and the right-hand wall has been plain since v3.3: measured at mid-thickness the four side walls hold 1 USB opening, 0 voids, 0 voids and the d28 bore. The fan is mounted blowing IN and its own 30 × 30 frame spans the bore, so the fan is part of the closure. Cooling is now carried by the skin of the box: 2.7 K above ambient at 1.6 W, 1.8 K once the fan’s through-flow is added (0.25 L/s at 12.2 Pa through the plate seam), against a 12 K ceiling — v3.3 measured 1.6 K, so the twelve hollows were worth 0.2 K — all four of those numbers measured in physics-audit section 4, none of them argued in prose
  • every module screwed down, RC522 included: 4 × M2.5 into blind pilots, heads sunk in the ring, no glue, no clips, no snap-fits
